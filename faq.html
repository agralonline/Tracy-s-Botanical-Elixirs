Frequently Asked Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship from our US fulfillment center within 1–2 business days. Domestic orders typically arrive within 3–7 business days; international delivery times vary by destination and are shown at checkout.

Are your ingredients organic and cruelty-free?

Yes. Every product is formulated with certified organic botanical extracts, is never tested on animals, and is free of synthetic fillers. Look for the Organic, Vegan, and Cruelty-Free badges on each product page.

What is your return policy?

Unopened products can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Opened products can only be returned if damaged or defective. See our Shipping & Returns page for full details.

Do I need an account to place an order?

No account or login is required. Checkout is handled securely by Stripe as a guest — just your email and shipping address.

Is my payment information secure?

Yes. All payments are processed directly by Stripe, a PCI-compliant payment processor. We never see or store your card details.

Can I use essential oils if I'm pregnant or have a medical condition?

Please consult a physician before use if you are pregnant, nursing, or managing a medical condition. Always perform a patch test before first use.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes, we ship worldwide. Shipping cost and estimated delivery time are calculated at checkout based on your destination; the customer is responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.
