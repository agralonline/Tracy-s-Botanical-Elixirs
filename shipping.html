Shipping & Returns

This policy is provided in English. The English version governs.

Shipping

Orders ship from our fulfillment center in the United States within 1–2 business days. Domestic US orders over $75 ship free; all other US orders ship at a flat rate calculated at checkout. International orders are welcome — shipping cost and estimated delivery time are calculated at checkout based on destination, and the customer is responsible for any customs duties or import taxes.

Returns

Unopened products may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund. Due to the nature of essential oils and skincare, opened products can only be returned if damaged or defective. To start a return, contact support@tracyusa.com with your order number.

Damaged or Missing Items

If your order arrives damaged or incomplete, reach out within 7 days of delivery and we'll arrange a replacement or refund at no cost to you.
