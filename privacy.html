Information We Collect

Account and order details you provide (name, email, shipping address); payment information, which is transmitted directly to and processed by Stripe and never stored on our servers; your selected storefront language, stored locally in your browser; and standard technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) collected via server logs and analytics.

To fulfill and ship orders, provide customer support, send order confirmations and (if you opt in) marketing emails, and improve the site.

Third Parties

We share data only as needed with Stripe (payments), our shipping carriers, and Firebase/Google Cloud (site data and hosting infrastructure). We do not sell personal data.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact privacy@tracyusa.com to make a request.
