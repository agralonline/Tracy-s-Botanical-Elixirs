Our Story

Tracy's Botanical Elixirs was founded on a simple belief: skincare and aromatherapy should feel like a ritual, not a routine. Every bottle we pour is a small act of craft — sourced from certified organic farms, distilled and blended in small batches, and finished by hand.

We ship from the United States to customers worldwide, and we've built our storefront in 24 languages because a beautiful ritual shouldn't be limited by geography. Wherever you are, the experience — and the purity of what's inside the bottle — stays exactly the same.

No fillers. No synthetic shortcuts. No animal testing. Just pure botanical extracts, thoughtfully sourced and honestly labeled, poured into glass designed to be kept on your vanity, not hidden in a drawer.
